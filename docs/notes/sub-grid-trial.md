# Sub-grid trial (seam)

Moved from the `SLICES.md` backlog on 2026-11-03; the backlog keeps a short pitch.

**Sub-grid trial.** Added 2026-11-03 at the user's request, from the same review. The data model is ready (`Universe` holds grids by `GridId`), but every physics path assumes one axis-aligned main grid: player collision, creatures, items, aiming, the edge guard. Collision against a moving, rotated grid is the hard part, and each new movement or creature feature adds to what would change. A small trial before much more physics piles up: one rotating platform the player can stand on and be carried by (riders kept in the grid's local space, per the doc), with blocks on it editable. Do it whenever a toy wants something that moves (an elevator, a swinging door as a moving piece, a cart).
- *Cows as sub-grids?* (the user's idea, 2026-11-03.) Cows are skinned meshes with bones, not boxels, so they aren't sub-grids in the doc's sense, and making one would mean a cow built of boxels. But part of the idea fits: standing on or riding a cow is the "carried by a moving frame" half of the trial, without rotated-grid collision. Options for the plan: ride a cow as a first step (moving collider, local-space rider), or a boxel-built creature (a golem or a cart-like beast) as a true sub-grid whose blocks can be hit off. The platform stays the clean test of grid collision.
