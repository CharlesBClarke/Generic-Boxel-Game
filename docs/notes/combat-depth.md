Moved from the `SLICES.md` backlog on 2026-11-03; the backlog keeps a short pitch.

**Combat depth.** Added 2026-10-31, deferred from Combat feel. Ideas for a higher skill ceiling, to try once enemies, tools and movement give combat something to be tuned against: swing direction from the mouse during the wind-up (sweep sideways, slam down, launch up), air combos with growing knockback, a sweet spot at the club's full reach, cancelling the recovery into a jump or dash, a charged heavy swing with a perfect-release window (the robot's spring), and what the guard does once something hits back. Also the Doom loop: close kills restoring health once the player has some.
- *Player wall slams* (2026-11-03, at the user's request). Cows take slam damage when knocked into a wall (`slam_speed`, `slam_damage` in `targets.toml`), but the player only takes fall damage: thrown into the fence by a charge at about 12 m/s, they just stop. Proposed: `HealthDef::{slam_speed, slam_damage}` with the cows' formula, `(speed − slam_speed) × slam_damage`, using the speed into a wall reported by the movement controller (like `last_landing_speed`); the camera jolts sideways like the robot's shock absorber bottoming out, and the readout says `SLAM -4`. Makes position matter: a fence at your back is a risk. Watch the numbers: a charge already does 13, so a slam on top could knock out from full health; start `slam_speed` high.
