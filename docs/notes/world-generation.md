# World generation

Moved from the `SLICES.md` backlog on 2026-11-03; the backlog keeps a short pitch.

World generation
- *Staging* (2026-11-03, discussed with the user). Nothing in the backlog strictly needs it yet, but Acoustics and stored light want caves, Liquid rule sets want terrain, Creatures on terrain and Covered blocks wait on it, and Generated detail's patch tint and Shading's sun show best on hills; the first of those picked is the likely trigger. Likely after the WebAssembly behavior seam, or sooner if the pasture gets tiresome. It's more than a generator: (1) the generator, base-mod content through the API, replacing the one-off `build_world` with a pure, repeatable "fill this chunk from a seed" (parallel, later WebAssembly or GPU); (2) chunk streaming, loading and unloading around the player with background meshing (today the whole test area loads at once), probably the bigger job; (3) then saving and LOD as they start to hurt. Also a design question first: the doc's "terrain that changes gameplay" (what terrains exist and what they do to movement, building and fighting). Suggested slices: a small generator with no streaming (a fixed area around spawn, maybe 512×512, hills and caves from a seed, loaded at once), then streaming once walking off the edge annoys.
- *Creatures on terrain* (2026-11-03, from Fighting cow, at the user's request). The cow fight was built for the flat pasture. The user expects terrain with more detail than Minecraft's (slabs, stairs and crafted shapes, down to texels), which cuts both ways: half-height steps make more ground walkable with the cow's 0.6 step height, but there are more small obstacles to catch on, and any pathfinding has to understand shaped cells, not just full blocks. When world generation arrives, check:
  - No pathfinding, and full blocks can't be stepped: a ledge between player and cow ends the fight, since the cow gives up. Fixes, by value: let creatures hop up a block, like Minecraft mobs; make an angry cow wait below or circle instead of leaving, so standing on a block isn't a free escape (the user wants herd fights to be challenging); then short-range pathfinding as an engine service.
  - *2026-11-06, Cow pathfinding:* the ledge item is done: cows hop a block, path round walls (shaped cells included) and wait below when there's no way up. Charges run off cliffs and falls hurt, so the edge-guard and fall-damage items are done too.
  - No line of sight: cows notice, defend and chase through hills and walls.
  - Charges are straight lines: trees and rocks stun them often. Probably good, terrain as a tool against the cow.
  - The edge guard is off while fighting, so a charging cow runs off cliffs, and creatures take no fall damage.
  - Knockback near drops: a charge throws the player about 4 m up and 12 m/s out, which off a ledge becomes heavy fall damage. Good danger, worth keeping.
- *Stage 1 done* (2026-11-05, Generated terrain). Generators are pure per-chunk functions (`api::terrain`), filled on all cores by `sim::terrain`; the client meshes on all cores at startup. Measured on the user's machine (8 cores, Iris Xe): 1024³ draws 9.85M triangles at about 25 fps using 2.1 GB, but meshing it at startup takes 20 s. So the next limit is startup and meshing, not drawing: streaming (generate and mesh in the background around the player, unload far chunks) is the natural next stage, with greedy meshing as a cheaper first cut at mesh cost. Things streaming must handle: `Welcome` sends every chunk at once; waypoints and creature spots on the terrain are computed from the default tuning; edits in generated chunks are lost on regenerate (and would be on unload, until saving).
- *Stage 2 done* (2026-11-11, Terrain streaming). Endless terrain in columns around the player, generated, lit and meshed in the background, edits kept in memory and saved; startup is quick and flying at 30 m/s keeps up. Left for stage 3: writing unloaded edits to disk (today they live in memory until a save), LOD (the full 512 m view is 1.6–2M triangles and 19–32 ms a frame on the Iris Xe), loading by cube once terrain is deep or tall, and saving creatures. The 1 km clear zone around the mods' built area (`sim::stream::CLEAR_DISTANCE`) goes once the test area is a saved place rather than built at startup (the user: "we area going to not have this issue when we impiment saved chunks anyway"). The headless walk and fly harness (`client::meshing::stream_timing`, ignored tests) measures streaming; the bench doesn't.
